How to use these prompts with NotebookLM

  1. Open NotebookLM and upload your chapter or study material as a source.
  2. Copy the prompt below (Single MCQ or Case-based) using the Copy button.
  3. Paste it into the NotebookLM chat and press Enter.
  4. Copy the output table that NotebookLM generates.
  5. Come back here → go to the Single MCQ or Case-based tab → paste → Start Test.
